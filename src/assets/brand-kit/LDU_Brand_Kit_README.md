# LDU · La De Unique — Brand Kit

## Logo files (SVG, vector, editable)
- `LDU_logo_onyx_on_transparent.svg` — primary logo, black text, transparent background (use on light backgrounds)
- `LDU_logo_ivory_on_transparent.svg` — reversed (white/ivory) logo, transparent background (use on dark backgrounds)
- `LDU_logo_onyx_on_ivory.svg` — primary logo on ivory background
- `LDU_logo_ivory_on_onyx.svg` — reversed logo on onyx (black) background
- `LDU_logo_onyx_on_fire.svg` — logo on the Fire Orange accent color

## Ibex emblem (secondary mark — do not use as a logo replacement)
- `LDU_ibex_onyx.svg` — black, transparent background
- `LDU_ibex_ivory.svg` — ivory/white, transparent background
- `LDU_ibex_fire.svg` — fire orange, transparent background
- `LDU_ibex_onyx_on_ivory.svg` / `LDU_ibex_ivory_on_onyx.svg` — with solid background fills

Onyx and Ivory carry ~80% of any composition. Fire Orange is the only accent color and should never exceed ~10% of a layout. Slate, Sand, and Dune are supporting tones for depth/print warmth — they never carry the logo.

### Pattern-layer colors (Sadu / Al-Qatt artwork only — never the logo)

Qatt: #C0392B, #3E7D4F, #2F5C8A, #8A5A72, #D2A72B

## Typography
- Display / wordmark: **Archivo** (variable font, pulled narrow "wdth 68", heavy "wght 850")
- Arabic display: **Aref Ruqaa**
- Arabic UI/body: **IBM Plex Sans Arabic**
- Arabic print-only: **Noto Kufi Arabic**
- Monospace / tracked labels (e.g. "LA DE UNIQUE"): **IBM Plex Mono**

These fonts aren't embedded in the SVGs — they're free on Google Fonts. If a recipient doesn't have Archivo installed, most renderers will fall back to a heavy grotesk (Arial Black/Helvetica Neue), which is a safe substitute.

## Logo rules (quick version)
- Keep the logo flat and high-contrast; let the wordmark carry the layout on its own.
- The ibex sits inline with "LDU," scaled to the letters' cap-height, standing in for the old dot — never as a large separate shape beside the name.
- Never use the ibex as a substitute for the wordmark, and never compress, stretch, or re-track the wordmark.
- Ibex emblem should never be a favicon and never packed with scattered marks.
