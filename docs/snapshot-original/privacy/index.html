Governance

Privacy notice

This notice describes how LDU handles information submitted through this site. It is informational, not legal advice.

Updated September 2026 · Riyadh · Saudi Arabia

Who we are

LDU is an entertainment house based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. This digital house is an editorial and intake surface for professional inquiries.

What we collect

If you use project intake, we process the fields you choose to provide: identity, organisation, role, work email, market, budget band, timeline, objective and context. A language preference may be stored on your device. We do not request national ID numbers, payment cards, or special-category data through this form.

Where it lives

Until a production intake endpoint is connected, submissions remain on your device (browser storage). They are not transmitted to LDU servers from this preview house. Direct-line contact uses your own telephone application.

Why we process it

To qualify whether a brief is a fit, to respond, and to protect the house from spam. Processing is based on your consent and on legitimate interest in answering a professional inquiry you initiated.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not run advertising pixels from this house. Third parties are used only if you choose to leave (Instagram) or place a phone call.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, including the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction. Contact the direct line and identify the inquiry. Do not send sensitive attachments unsolicited.

Reference

For the Kingdom’s official data-governance materials see the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA) public resources. This page does not replace those texts.
