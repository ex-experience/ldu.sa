Privacy Notice

Last updated: 11 September 2026.

What this notice covers

This website may collect information you submit through the project enquiry form and, only with your consent, analytics data used to understand site performance.

Project enquiries

Information may include your name, organization, work email, phone number, project type, budget range, timeline, decision role and project description. This information should be used only to assess and respond to your enquiry.

Analytics and pixels

Non-essential analytics and advertising pixels remain disabled until you choose to allow them. Essential storage may be used to remember your privacy choice.

Data minimization

Do not submit confidential contracts, financial data, identity documents or special-category personal data through the public form.

Your choices

You may decline analytics and still use the website. You may also contact LDU directly by phone for a project enquiry.

Saudi PDPL

This notice is designed as a practical website disclosure and does not replace legal review. Before commercial launch, LDU should have Saudi counsel or a qualified privacy professional validate controller details, retention periods, lawful bases, cross-border transfer language, and data-subject request procedures under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law and its implementing regulations.
