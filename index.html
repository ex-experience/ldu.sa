02POSITION

Entertainment can arrive from outside. It can be built from inside. Neither belongs automatically.

LDU works at that seam: between brands, institutions, entertainment properties, talent and audiences. We challenge the brief, uncover the more valuable opportunity underneath it, tune it to the culture it must live in, connect the people who can make it real, then shape the story and the stage through which it reaches the world.

03THE UNCOMFORTABLE QUESTIONS

The questions are not a warm-up to the work. They frequently are the work.

01

What is this actually for?

A real objective, or a budget with a deadline attached?

02

Who is it for, and what do they leave with?

An audience, or only a target number?

03

Does it belong here, or was it just brought here?

Culture coded in, or applied later as decoration?

04

What survives after they leave?

Something durable, or something that ends when the lights go off?
