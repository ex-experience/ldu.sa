THE POSITION

Entertainment can arrive from outside. It can be built from inside. Neither belongs automatically.

LDU works at that seam: between brands, institutions, entertainment properties, talent and audiences. We challenge the brief, uncover the more valuable opportunity underneath it, tune it to the culture it must live in, connect the people who can make it real, then shape the story and the stage through which it reaches the world.
